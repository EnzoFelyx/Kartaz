# How to play Kartaz

Kartaz is a map exploration game where you hunt collectible cards of football players. You don't need to leave home: you "walk" by dragging the map. Along the way you find cards, coins, batteries and flags, build your team and trade cards with other players.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. **Sign in** as a guest, or with your Google or Apple account. You can start as a guest and link your account later in your Profile, so you don't lose your progress.
2. **Welcome pack:** after walking your first 10 meters on the map, you get 5 cards. They become your first team.
3. **Explore:** zoom in on the map until **Explore Mode** turns on, and start walking.

---

## 2. The map and Explore Mode

- The world is split into **areas** (hexagons). Each area has its own cards, coins, batteries and one flag.
- **Explore Mode** turns on when you zoom in close. While it's on:
  - **dragging the map = walking.** Your position is always the center of the screen;
  - you see the items and other players in the area;
  - walking uses energy and gives XP.
- When zoomed out, you move freely around the map without using energy, but you don't see any game content either. Use it to pick where to go.
- **Map panel:** shows the place, the area's difficulty, how many players are **nearby**, how many cards are in the area, who owns the flag, and the radar button.

---

## 3. Energy

Energy is the fuel for exploring.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Max energy | 100% |
| Walking | uses 1% per km |
| Battery on the map | recharges 5% |
| Fill the tank with coins | 50 coins |

- With no energy you can't walk: the map snaps back to where you were.
- You can only pick up batteries when your tank is **not** full.

---

## 4. What you find on the map

To pick up any item, just **walk over it**. Collection is automatic.

### Cards
- Each area draws its cards **every 15 minutes**. All players see the same draw.
- Many windows have no cards at all (about 70%). When they do, there can be 1, 2, 3 or, with a lot of luck, 5.
- You only see cards that are **very close to you**: they show up as a face-down card that shakes and makes your phone vibrate lightly.
- When you walk over one, the **"Player found!"** screen opens. Tap to reveal the card.
- If nobody picks up a card before the 15-minute window ends, it disappears and goes back into the draw.

### Coins
- Worth 1 to 5 each, and they go straight to your wallet.

### Batteries
- Recharge some of your energy.

### Discovery flag
- Each area has **one** flag, in its center.
- The **first** player to reach it becomes the **area owner** and earns **+50 XP**. Your name shows up on the panel for everyone who passes by.

---

## 5. Radar

- Reveals **every card in the whole area**, not only the ones near you.
- Costs **10% energy** and has a **5-minute cooldown**.
- When used, the map zooms out to show the area, displays the cards for 3 seconds, then goes back. Memorize where they are and go get them.

---

## 6. Packs

| Pack | Cards | How often |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | 5 | once |
| Daily | 2 | every 24 h |

Packs are delivered after you walk **10 meters** on the map. Cards are revealed one by one.

---

## 7. The cards

- Each card is a **unique copy** of a player, with a serial number such as `669 / 1000`. Nobody else has that copy.
- **Rarity**, by the player's overall:

| Rarity | Overall |
|---|---|
| Legendary | 90 or more |
| Epic | 87 to 89 |
| Rare | 84 to 86 |
| Common | below 84 |

- **Serial class**, by the copy's number:

| Class | Which copies | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Extreme | #1 and the last one | gold |
| Elite | the first ones (up to 1% of the print run, at least the first 3) | purple |
| Standard | all the others | blue |

So a common card with serial `1 / 1000` is still special.

---

## 8. Collection

- **Collection:** the full player catalog, as a grid, a list or by national team, with search. Shows what you have and what's missing.
- **My Collection:** only your cards, with filters for overall, national team, position and source (pack or map).
- **Card details:** stats, serial number, date and place collected. You can also favorite it and open **global discovery**, which shows who has already found copies of that player.

---

## 9. Your team

You pick **5 starters**. Your team's strength is its **team overall**:

> team overall = sum of the starters' overalls × (1 + bonus)

**Chemistry bonus**: players from the same national team or the same club among your starters.

| Matching players | National team bonus | Club bonus |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | +0.625% | +0.5% |
| 3 | +1.25% | +1% |
| 4 or 5 | +3.75% | +3% |

The two bonuses **stack**. Tap **Auto** and the game builds the best lineup from your cards. The **"?"** shows these rules inside the app.

> Starters **can't** be offered in trades. To trade one, take it out of your team first.

---

## 10. Trades

1. Open a card and tap **global discovery** (found copies) to see who owns copies of that player.
2. Pick the copy you want and the cards you'll offer. It can be several for several.
3. Send the proposal. It stays **Pending** until the other player accepts or declines. You can cancel it at any time.
4. Once accepted, the trade happens instantly and you get a notification.

The **Trades** tab shows **Pending** and **Accepted** proposals.

---

## 11. XP and level

| Action | XP |
|---|---|
| Walk 1 km | +10 |
| Take a discovery flag | +50 |
| Capture an area | +10 |

Your level goes up with XP, and each level needs a bit more than the last (level 1→2: 100 XP; 5→6: 1,120 XP; 10→11: 3,160 XP). The max level is 100.

---

## 12. Tips to get the most out of the game

- **Open the app every day:** the daily pack gives you 2 free cards every 24 h. Remember it only arrives after you walk 10 m.
- **Explore new areas:** each discovery flag is worth 50 XP, the same as walking 5 km. Areas nobody has reached yet are the best way to level up, and they put your name on the map.
- **Use the radar wisely:** it costs 10% energy, so use it when the panel shows there are cards in the area. If the area has 0 cards, save your energy.
- **Watch the clock:** cards change every 15 minutes. If an area is empty, walk to the next one or wait for the next window.
- **Grab batteries and coins on the way:** they're your energy reserve. 50 coins fill your tank.
- **Don't waste batteries:** they can't be picked up with a full tank, so leave them for after you've walked a bit.
- **Travel zoomed out:** to cover long distances without using energy, zoom out, move the map, and only zoom in to turn on Explore Mode when you arrive.
- **Build chemistry:** 4 players from the same national team and the same club give almost 7% bonus. Sometimes a lower-rated player is worth more in your team because of chemistry.
- **Trade smart:** duplicates and cards outside your team make great trade offers. Look for low serial numbers (elite and extreme), which are the rarest.
- **Link your account:** if you play as a guest, link Google or Apple in your Profile so you don't lose your collection when you switch phones.
